What it can and can't do

Works great for

  • Changing dates, job titles, phone numbers, emails and skills
  • Fixing typos and rewording a line
  • Making text shorter, or a little longer if there's room on the line

Can't do (yet)

  • Add or remove lines, bullets or sections
  • Wrap text onto a new line, or grow boxes and tags to fit longer text
  • Edit scanned resumes (pictures of text)

If an edit can't be done cleanly, the editor tells you and leaves your file untouched.
